## About This Event

Pull on your favorite pajamas and come wind down with us! Storytime Pajama Night is a cozy drop-in evening at The Wandering Lantern where children ages 3–8 settle in for three picture books read aloud by lantern light — with warm hot cocoa to hold in their hands.

It's a lovely way to ease into bedtime mode together as a community, swapping the bustle of the day for a quiet, story-filled evening in the bookshop.

### What to Expect

Kids arrive in their PJs and get cozy as our reader brings three picture books to life by lantern light. The intimate setting makes the stories feel a little more magical — and the hot cocoa doesn't hurt either.

Reading aloud is one of the most powerful ways to nurture early literacy, and evenings like this one give children a chance to practice listening, experience the joy of a great story, and share something special with the families around them.

### Details

- **Ages:** 3–8
- **Format:** Drop-in (no registration required)
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Location:** The Wandering Lantern, Lakewood, OH

No supplies needed — just pajamas and a love of stories!
