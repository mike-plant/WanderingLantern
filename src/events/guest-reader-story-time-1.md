Join us on October 3 for a lively, interactive story time with Cleveland-area children’s author, educator, and dancer Terese M. G.!

Terese founded Movement Joy Company, a movement-enrichment program that serves students of all ages and abilities during the school day. After seeing how powerfully movement and imagination support children’s growth, she began composing what she calls “movement poetry”—playful stories designed to get children listening, imagining, and moving.

Featuring vibrant illustrations and large, accessible print, Terese’s books are created for children, families, and classrooms to enjoy together. Her writing has brought joy to students for more than 20 years. Terese considers herself a “joy spreader,” and we can’t wait to welcome her to The Wandering Lantern for this special story time!
