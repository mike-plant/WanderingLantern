**The Santa Experience at The Wandering Lantern 🎄🎅✨**

Step into a little Christmas magic at The Wandering Lantern!

We’re partnering with **Wildflower by Abbey** to create a cozy and memorable Santa Experience for families. Children will have the chance to meet Santa and have professional photos taken with a charming holiday backdrop designed to feel warm, festive, and storybook-inspired.

While families wait for their turn, kids can enjoy a simple Christmas craft, write a letter to Santa, browse our favorite holiday books, and soak up all the cozy bookstore Christmas atmosphere.

Families participating in the Santa Experience will also receive a special Wandering Lantern offer to use while they shop, making it the perfect time to pick up a Christmas book, stocking stuffer, or gift for someone you love.

Come dressed in your Christmas best, bring your wish list, and make a sweet holiday memory with us.

**Professional photography by Wildflower by Abbey.**
