We're excited to welcome children's book author **Bonnie Kostan of Quill Town Books** to The Wandering Lantern for a special Saturday Story Time!

Bonnie is an award-winning children's author whose stories encourage imagination, kindness, and learning through playful storytelling. Her books include _Put the Phone Down, Mary Lou_, _Kendra's New Shoes_, and _Count to 20 With the Silly Dinosaurs_.

Join us for a cozy morning as Bonnie shares her stories and introduces our little readers to the wonderful world of children's books. Come meet the author, discover some new favorites, and celebrate the joy of reading together!

**Saturday, December 5 | 11:00 AM**

**Free and open to all families!**
