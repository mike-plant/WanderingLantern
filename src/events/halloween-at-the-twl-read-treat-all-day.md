🎃 **Halloween at TWL: Read & Treat All Day!** 👻📚
**Saturday, October 31 | All Day**

Come spend Halloween at The Wandering Lantern! We're filling the bookstore with cozy, silly, just-spooky-enough fun for little readers and their families.

Wear your costume and stop in anytime throughout the day for **Read & Treat** fun! Hunt your way through the shelves with our Halloween bookstore scavenger hunt, make something spooky at the craft table, enjoy Halloween stories, snap a festive photo, and pick up a treat while you're here.

Of course, we'll have plenty of Halloween, fall, magical, mysterious, and delightfully spooky books waiting to be discovered, too!

🍬 Treats for little readers
👻 Halloween bookstore scavenger hunt
✂️ Drop-in Halloween crafts
📚 Spooky & silly stories
📸 Halloween photo spot
🎭 Costumes encouraged
🎃 Halloween surprises throughout the store

**Free • Family Friendly • No Registration Required**

Come for a few minutes or stay and play awhile. Make The Wandering Lantern one of your family's Halloween stops and **Read & Treat with us!**
