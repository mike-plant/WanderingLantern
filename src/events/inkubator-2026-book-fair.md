We’re excited to share that The Wandering Lantern has been invited to host a table at **Literary Cleveland’s 2026 Inkubator Writing Conference**!

Inkubator is one of the country’s largest free writing conferences, bringing writers and readers together for workshops, craft talks, panel discussions, readings, and community connection. The in-person portion of this year’s conference will take place at the Cleveland Public Library on **September 11–12**.

Stop by the book fair to The Wandering Lantern’s table to say hello, browse books, and talk with us about stories, writing, and raising the next generation of readers and creators. We’re honored to be part of this special celebration of Cleveland’s literary community and hope to see some familiar faces there!
