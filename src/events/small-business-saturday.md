Celebrate Small Business Saturday and The Wandering Lantern’s anniversary weekend with local author and illustrator Bill Cotter!

Bill is the creator of the playful and enormously popular _Don’t Push the Button!_ series, which invites young readers to interact with the story and discover what happens when that very tempting button gets pushed.

Visit us on Saturday, November 28, to meet Bill, chat with him about his stories and illustrations, purchase books from the series, and have your copies personally signed.

Small Business Saturday is a wonderful opportunity to shop locally and support the independent businesses that make Lakewood special. When you choose a neighborhood bookstore, you help preserve the kind of welcoming, imaginative space families want in their community. When you purchase the work of a local author and illustrator, you’re supporting Lakewood’s creative community, too!

Come celebrate independent books, local creativity, and another year of The Wandering Lantern. We’re so grateful to be part of this community!
