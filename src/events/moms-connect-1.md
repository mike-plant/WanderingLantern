## Moms Connect

Having little ones at home can be wonderful — and sometimes really lonely. **Moms Connect** is a relaxed gathering created to give moms with children ages 0–4 a reason to get out of the house, meet other parents, and enjoy some grown-up connection.

Bring your friends or come by yourself and make some new ones. We’ll have snacks, space for little ones to play and explore, and plenty of time to talk, laugh, share stories, and connect with people who understand this particular season of life.

There’s no pressure and no formal agenda — just a welcoming place to show up as you are, little ones and all.

**For moms with children ages 0–4.**
