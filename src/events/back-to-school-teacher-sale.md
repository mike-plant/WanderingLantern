## Back-to-School Teacher Sale

**August 11–15**

Teachers, librarians, homeschool educators, and school staff are invited to save **20% on all in-stock books** during our Back-to-School Teacher Sale!

Whether you’re refreshing your classroom library, searching for the perfect read-aloud, or adding books that help every student feel seen, we’d love to help you find something special for the new school year.

Bring your school ID or another form of educator verification to receive the discount.

_Valid on in-stock books only. Excludes special orders, consignment titles, and previously discounted merchandise._
