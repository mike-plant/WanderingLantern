**Don't Push the Button! An Interactive Story Time with Bill Cotter**

Whatever you do... DON'T PUSH THE BUTTON!

We're thrilled to welcome author and illustrator **Bill Cotter** to The Wandering Lantern for a special Small Business Saturday appearance as part of our **One Year of Wonder anniversary celebration!**

Bill is the creator of the wonderfully silly and interactive _Don't Push the Button!_ series, featuring Larry the Monster.

Join us as Bill shares some of his favorite stories and invites little readers to participate in the fun! After story time, kids can get creative with a make-your-own-button art project and learn how to draw Larry the Monster himself.

We'll also be celebrating the holiday season with Bill's festive books, _Don't Push the Button! A Christmas Adventure_ and _Don't Shake the Present_, along with his popular _Don't Draw in This Book!_

Come meet the author, discover his books, and enjoy a morning of stories, art, laughter, and a little mischief!

**Saturday, November 28 | 11 AM- 2PM**

Part of The Wandering Lantern's First Anniversary Celebration!
