**Uptown Lakewood Trick or Treat 🎃👻**
Grab your costumes, your treat bags, and the whole family for one of our favorite fall traditions! The **Uptown Lakewood Business Alliance** is bringing its annual Trick or Treat back to Madison Avenue, with participating neighborhood businesses opening their doors to costumed kids for an evening of treats and community fun.

Make **The Wandering Lantern** one of your stops! Come inside, warm up, browse some wonderfully spooky and seasonal books, collect a treat, and enjoy a little Halloween fun with us.

This is such a fun way to explore Madison Avenue, meet your neighborhood businesses, and celebrate Halloween together right here in Lakewood. Come trick-or-treat your way down the street and support the local businesses that make our community special!

**Costumes encouraged. Families welcome. Trick-or-treating takes place from 5–7:30 PM.**
