## Cleveland's First Dedicated Children's Bookstore Opens in Lakewood

**Lakewood, OH** – November 29, 2025 – The Wandering Lantern, Cleveland's first dedicated children's bookstore, will officially open its doors at 15729 Madison Avenue in Lakewood. The store marks a significant addition to Cleveland's independent bookstore scene, offering a curated selection of children's literature in a magical, welcoming environment.

Founded by Emily Mitchell-Polci, a writer and former educator with a Master's in English Literature, and Michael Plant, a local realtor and entrepreneur, The Wandering Lantern aims to become a community gathering place where families can discover quality children's books and participate in literary events.

"There hasn't been a dedicated children's bookstore in Cleveland, and we saw an opportunity to create something truly special for families to enjoy together," says Mitchell. "Every book in our store is handpicked to inspire wonder, creativity, and a love of reading."

The 1,500-square-foot store features cozy reading nooks, carefully curated book collections for all ages, and a dedicated event space for story times and author visits. The store will host regular programming including weekly story hours, creative events, seasonal celebrations, and visits from local and nationally recognized children's book authors.

"Thriving neighborhoods need gathering places where people connect," adds Plant. "We're committed to making The Wandering Lantern a welcoming space for Lakewood families and the broader Cleveland community."

The Wandering Lantern joins Lakewood's vibrant Madison Avenue business district, contributing to the area's growing reputation as a destination for independent retail and dining.

For more information, visit thewanderinglantern.com or follow @thewanderinglanterncle on Instagram and @thewanderinglantern on Facebook.

**About The Wandering Lantern**
The Wandering Lantern is Cleveland's first dedicated children's bookstore, offering a curated selection of picture books, middle grade novels, young adult and even some books for grown ups. Located in Lakewood's Madison Avenue district, the store serves as a community hub for families seeking quality children's literature and literary programming.
